ABOUT USI

About the Initiative

The Unified Systems Initiative (USI) is an independent software development initiative focused on operating systems, computing technologies and software experimentation.

USI provides an environment for exploring ideas, developing independent projects and experimenting with different approaches to computing.

OUR OBJECTIVES

Objectives

Our work is centered around independent development and the exploration of computing systems.

  • Develop independent software projects.
  • Explore operating system architecture.
  • Experiment with computing technologies.
  • Document development and technical research.
OUR APPROACH

Independent Development

USI values experimentation, technical curiosity and original ideas. Projects are developed independently, with an emphasis on understanding how systems work and exploring how they can be built.

Our Philosophy

Independent development.
Original ideas.
Systems built with purpose.
